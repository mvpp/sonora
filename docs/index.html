Pluggable engines

Gemini, Mistral (Voxtral), OpenAI, Groq, any compatible endpoint, or local Whisper.

Hesitation cleanup

An optional pass removes "uh", false starts and repetitions, fixes misheard words, and rejoins sentences split by a pause.

Reformulation prompts

Transform a dictation through an LLM: formal tone, terminal command, professional rewrite…

History

Theme & waveform

Three steps, that's all

From install to your first dictation in under two minutes.

Install & choose an engine

Download Sonora, open the Settings (⚙ icon on the bar), pick a provider and paste your API key (or point to a local Whisper model).

Press the shortcut

The floating bar appears and dictation starts. A sound wave confirms Sonora hears you.

Text lands at your cursor

Release: the text is typed where you were working, or copied to the clipboard. Reformulate it if needed with your prompts.
